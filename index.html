Executive Technology Overview

The Standardized External Mandibular Advancement Device (eMAD®) represents a clinical and mechanical paradigm shift in obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) therapy. Designed specifically to circumvent the structural limitations of conventional intraoral appliances, eMAD® shifts the therapeutic force mechanics entirely outside the oral cavity, establishing immediate upper airway patency without relying on patient dentition or custom laboratory fabrication.

5 Core Disruptive USPs

1. Teeth-Independent Mechanics

Fully compatible with edentulous and partially edentulous patient populations—a critical, high-revenue OSA demographic segment previously entirely excluded from dental-anchored therapy.

2. Non-Mouth-Locking System

Engineered with dynamic extraoral vector force, allowing patients to freely speak and drink water while wearing the device, eradicating chronic intraoral lock-in discomfort and TMJ strain.

3. Immediate Dispensing Ready

Standardized, scalable industrial architecture across three predefined sizes (S, M, L). Completely bypasses custom dental laboratory wait times, allowing immediate clinical fulfillment.

4. Scalable Upgrade Potential

Unique biomechanical framework capable of upgrading into an extraoral orthodontic appliance for treating mandibular retrognathia (Class II / recessed lower jaw) to correct facial aesthetics and airway anatomy.

5. Advanced Bio-Hygiene

Features a completely open extraoral framework, minimizing oral dental soreness, eliminating intraoral bacterial stagnation, and allowing effortless routine cleaning maintenance.
